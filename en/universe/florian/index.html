PORTRAIT — FLORIAN MALLET

THE MAN BEHIND
THE FREQUENCY.

Florian Mallet is a DJ, producer and founder of Electronic Show. His journey brings together more than twenty years of electronic culture, long-term professional experience and a personal story that has become inseparable from the return of ES.

TWO JOURNEYS, ONE METHOD

BUILDING TO LAST.

Outside Electronic Show, Florian has spent more than ten years with the same company and has worked as a team leader for six years. That experience shapes the way he builds ES: organising, anticipating, bringing people together and turning an idea into a lasting project.

RECONSTRUCTION

THE PAUSE THAT RELIT THE SIGNAL.

A long period away from work for health reasons forced Florian to slow down and rethink his priorities. It was precisely during this enforced pause that he decided to relaunch Electronic Show, initially to keep his mind active. The project quickly gathered momentum, became a full creative activity again and grew alongside his personal reconstruction.

THREE GUIDING VALUES

PERSISTENT.
DEMANDING.
LOYAL.

Three values that define both the man and the way he works. Electronic Show moves forward with a simple ambition: to build something honest, coherent and made to last.

NEXT CHAPTER
