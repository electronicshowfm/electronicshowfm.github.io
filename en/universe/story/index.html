ARCHIVE 01 — THE STORY

BEFORE THE SILENCE,
THE FREQUENCY WAS ALREADY THERE.

Electronic Show did not begin with its return. Its story has been unfolding since 2003 through clubs, radio shows, encounters and a passion for music that can endure through the years.

2003

THE FIRST SIGNALS

Electronic Show took shape around Florian Mallet, House Music and a simple ambition: to share a different selection, both in clubs and on the radio. The project gradually found its identity and its audience.

150+

A SHOW THAT TRAVELLED

Across more than 150 shows, the programme moved far beyond its starting point. Records kept at the time showed listeners in 32 countries — a historic reach from the project’s first life.

15 YEARS

THE SILENCE

For fifteen years, Florian’s professional and personal life led him towards other projects. Electronic Show went quiet, but music never truly disappeared: he missed mixing and the desire to share remained. A hard-drive crash later erased many files and statistics from that first era, but not its story.

RETURN

THE SIGNAL LIGHTS UP AGAIN

Fifteen years later, Electronic Show was reborn in a broader form: podcasts, original productions, news, Inside and collaborations. What began as a show became a universe.

FACES FROM THE ARCHIVES

CLUBS • PRIVATE EVENTS • JOURNEY

The archives are incomplete. The memories kept what matters most: Electronic Show already had a story before its return.
