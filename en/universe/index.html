TERMINAL 03 — UNIVERSE

BEFORE THE RETURN,
THERE WAS THE SIGNAL.

Electronic Show is a universe built around electronic music: DJ mixes, original productions, news, club culture and the stories behind the creative process. Born from a passion for House Music, radio and the stage, it now connects its history to a new generation of projects.

THE CREATOR

PROFESSIONAL & HUMAN

DJ and producer Florian Mallet created Electronic Show in the early 2000s. From clubs to internationally streamed radio shows, he developed a musical identity driven by House Music, the energy of the mix and a constant desire to share.

After fifteen years of silence, Florian switched the Signal back on with a more mature vision. This return is also part of a personal reconstruction: rediscovering creation and growing a project that has become a complete universe.

THE STORY

Electronic Show took shape in 2003 through clubs, DJ mixes and radio. More than 150 shows later, the programme had crossed borders, with listeners recorded in 32 countries. After fifteen years of silence, the Signal came back to life.
